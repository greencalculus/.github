# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with the number, not the person.

Most of what happens in these repositories is people telling us we got something
wrong — a factor, a boundary, a citation. That is the point of the project, and
it only works if being corrected is comfortable and correcting is safe. Assume
the other person is reading the same document you are and reached a different
conclusion honestly.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing your working — the publisher, the document, the cell
- Accepting a correction gracefully, and issuing one kindly
- Focusing on what is best for people relying on these numbers
- Being welcoming to people whose expertise is climate rather than code, or the
  other way round

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@greencalculus.com**. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that are not aligned to this code of conduct, and will give
reasons when they do.
